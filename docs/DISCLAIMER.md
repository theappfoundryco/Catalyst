# Important notice

Catalyst is a tool for diagnosing and tidying a developer's own machine. It is **not a security audit**, and it gives **no professional, legal or financial advice**.

- **Its findings can be wrong.** They come from automated checks of what the system reports. A clean report does not mean a machine is secure, healthy or free of problems, and a warning is a prompt to look, not a verdict.
- **Figures are estimates.** Drive wear, battery health, sizes and speeds come from what macOS, Linux and their tools report, and can differ from a manufacturer's own diagnostics.
- **It changes things only when you say yes.** Moving files to the Trash, editing a startup file, stopping a program, turning off a startup item, running a cleanup command or installing a helper each happens after you see it and agree. Some of these cannot be undone, and say so first. Read what is shown before you agree.
- **Admin steps are yours to approve.** When something needs admin rights, `sudo` asks for your password on your terminal. Catalyst never sees it. Approve only what you understand.
- **Keep backups.** Before any cleanup, this tool included, have a recent backup of anything you cannot replace.
- **Use it on machines you own or may administer,** and follow your employer's or school's rules for machines they manage.
